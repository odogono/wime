**The muster and defensive Demos fetch the sceptre.** Frodo picks it up on
the road to Rivendell and Merry shows it to Denethor, which brings Gondor in
before the war. Over 10,000 Classic seeds this raises muster's win rate from
46.9% to 49.1% and defensive's from 91.0% to 91.9% (#282).
