**Retreat leaves the fight for good; a Character who reaches the edge of the display cannot be killed, and any follower reverts to its Default Orders.**
If the retreating Character's side wins, it walks back on. If not, it reappears on the Campaign Level at the place of the fight and carries on with its Orders. Anyone who was following a Leader loses that link and goes back to Default Orders.
