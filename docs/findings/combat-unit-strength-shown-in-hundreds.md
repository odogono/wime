**Unit strengths in the combat window are probably shown in hundreds.**
The manual's example window shows Units as "20 Lt. Inf." and "20 Orcs". Units range from about a hundred to several thousand strong, so a figure of 20 most likely means 2,000. The manual does not say so; this is an inference.
