**Meetings now ask you what to do.** When two friendly Forces meet, a
window at the top of the map says who has met whom, as the original
words it ("FARAMIR meets EOMER." / "EOMER welcomes FARAMIR to join."),
and offers to follow, or to go there, or to continue on. The war waits
for your answer, and after about 14 seconds the window continues on by
itself (#70).
