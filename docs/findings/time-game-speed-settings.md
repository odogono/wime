**There are three Game Speeds, Normal, Hasty and Very Hasty, and the setting controls the rate of all actions in the game.**
The manual suggests Very Hasty at the start, slowing down later. Normal is needed during Encounters, because at faster speeds speech leaves the display too quickly to read. Elsewhere the manual describes a siege as lasting "many turns", which is loose wording: the game runs in real time, not in turns.
