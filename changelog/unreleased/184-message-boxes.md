**Message boxes open one at a time, in the order they are raised, and stay as long as the original's.** A box raised while another has just opened waits for it, the game standing still, instead of fights and meetings jumping the queue. Boxes without buttons stay up to about 24 seconds on the Campaign Level and 30 on the Animation Level unless a later one replaces them, leaving a Level closes its box, and the Full Map can't be opened under a box with buttons. A press off a box's buttons no longer closes it; a click on a Force or a Place replaces it, and N and Y answer it (#184).
