**River tiles are impassable away from a crossing, and a Force whose route meets a river with no crossing in reach walks up and down the bank forever, keeping its Order.**
Éomer, ordered from East Emnet (1360, 960) to Edoras (1248, 992), reaches the Entwash at x = 1328 and never crosses tiles 228, 244 and 247. Each day he walks north along the bank to about (1328, 945), returns south-east to about (1341, 960), heads west again, and rests each evening; after four days he is still looping with the destination set. The manual's named crossings (such as finding:map-fords-of-isen-only-isen-crossing) are the only places a river can be passed, and the route-finder does not search beyond the nearest bank for one.
