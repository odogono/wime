**A new Atlas map draws Middle Earth as an ink-and-parchment map in the
manner of Tolkien's own.** Choose Map: Atlas in the in-game menu. The map
zooms smoothly from the whole of Middle Earth down into the Campaign
Level, with woods, mountain ranges, rivers, roads, bridges and fords
drawn in ink, each Place as a small picture, and Forces as figures,
markers or dots by how far you zoom. Zooming out past the Campaign Level
takes you to the Full Map Level. Map: Classic keeps the original's look
(#182).
