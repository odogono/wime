**Combat is controlled through a window at the top right that lists the Good side in one column and the Evil side in the other, one row per Character or Unit.**
Each friendly row carries one of four options: Charge, Engage, Withdraw or Retreat. The manual's example lists Gandalf, Aragorn, Frodo, Sam, Merry, Pippin and "20 Lt. Inf." against "Nazgul Lord", "2nd Nazgul" and "20 Orcs".
