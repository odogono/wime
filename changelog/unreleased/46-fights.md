**Forces that meet an enemy now fight.** Evil and Good Leaders that come within reach of each other fight it out round by round, by the original's rules: Characters are wounded and killed, Units lose men and are destroyed, and followers of a fallen Leader carry on alone. Game time stands still during a fight. Until the combat window arrives, every fight is settled as if you had chosen to ignore it. Games saved before this release can't be restored (#46).
