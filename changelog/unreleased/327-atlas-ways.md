**The Atlas map shows where your Forces are heading.** While you give an
Order, a dotted line runs from the Force to the spot under the pointer,
through the river Crossing it would head for first. Open a Force's
window to see its way while it marches, and the line fades out after you
confirm an Order. Only your own Forces show theirs (#327).
