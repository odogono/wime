**The Council of Elrond is held when the Ring-bearer reaches Rivendell with Gandalf there.** The Fellowship forms under Gandalf, the hobbits and Aragorn are healed, the calendar moves on by up to two months, the Nazgûl go to sleep, and Bilbo can now be met at Rivendell. The Council's message holds the game for 24 seconds, as the original's did.
