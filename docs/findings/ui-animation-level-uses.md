**The Animation Level shows a single place with animated figures on digitised backgrounds, and is used for Encounters, for combat involving Characters, and for handling objects.**
Its icons are the eye (status), the map (up a Level) and the provisions (objects). Clicking a figure opens a window saying who it is and what it is doing.
