**A mixed Force travels at its Leader's speed class, not at the pace of its slowest member.**
Faramir (entry 15, class 3) leads his Rangers (entry 16, class 2) from North Ithilien to Osgiliath. After resting at (1648, 1141), the Force covers the remaining 27 pixels south and 16 pixels diagonally south-west in 31 steps, arriving with 1 of his 32 stamina left. That fits class 3 (about 31 steps at 414/256 straight and 292/256 per axis on the diagonal) and not class 2 (about 34 steps, more than his stamina), so the Rangers do not slow him. Followers hold no position of their own while following. This qualifies finding:movement-speed-classes, whose per-entry classes in a Force other than the Leader's do not set the Force's pace.
