**Friendly Forces now meet as they did in the original.**
When a moving Leader reaches a Leader that is not Evil, the game stops
for a meeting if one of them is mobilised: one may follow the other,
or an advisor such as Elrond invites it to talk. Following makes the
two one Force, and Gollum slips away from any Force led by a Wizard.
Until the meeting window arrives, every meeting answers "Continue on"
at once (#69).
