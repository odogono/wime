**The manual sketches where the free peoples and Saruman stand at the start: the Elves in Lórien, the Dwarves in the Iron Hills and the Lonely Mountain, Gondor and Rohan spread through their lands, and the Rangers at Bree.**
Several Units are gathered at Minas Tirith. Saruman holds Isengard and Orthanc with Orcs, Half-orcs and Dunlendings, which makes the Gap of Rohan dangerous. The exact starting positions have to come from the game data.
