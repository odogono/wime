**Attacking a fortress or other defended Place costs the attacker more; citadels protect better than ordinary defences, and walled cities such as Minas Tirith protect best of all.**
The same applies to Evil strongholds, so the player pays a price for attacking them too. The manual gives the order of strength but no numbers.
