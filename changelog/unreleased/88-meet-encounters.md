**Forces now meet the Encounters waiting across Middle-earth.** Orcs, trolls and balrogs attack a passing Force unless it evades them, and are never used up; Rangers, hobbits and other wanderers pass by the Character you watch on the Animation Level; and Círdan, Tom Bombadil, Galadriel, Treebeard and Radagast stop a Force to invite it to talk, each met once, save Radagast, who comes back. The talks and pass-by messages are shown in a later change (#88).
