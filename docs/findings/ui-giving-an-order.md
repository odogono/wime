**To give an Order, the player selects the arrow, clicks a Force, then clicks a destination, and everyone in the Force takes the Order.**
Clicking the Force opens a window at the top left listing who is selected and where each is heading. Followers are listed indented under the Character they follow. While a destination is being chosen, the arrow's shaft shows the word "TO".
