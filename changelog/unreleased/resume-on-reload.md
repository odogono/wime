**Reloading the page during a game takes you straight back into it.** The
game resumes from its autosave, paused under the in-game menu, instead of
starting over at the boot sequence. The in-game menu's dim now covers the
whole window over the Atlas map, and switching the map in Settings while
the menu is open no longer leaves the old map behind it.
