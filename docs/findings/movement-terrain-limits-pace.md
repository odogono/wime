**The speed-class pace applies on road and track tiles; off them a Force manages about 1 pixel a quarter-hour, whatever its class.**
On the Great East Road (tile 105 and its neighbours) Frodo's Force covers 1.31 pixels a tick, the class-1 rate from finding:movement-speed-classes, and on the Ithilien track (tile 104) Faramir covers 1.62 a tick straight and 2.28 Manhattan on a diagonal, the class-3 rates. Off such tiles the pace drops to about 1 pixel a tick, on a diagonal about 1 per axis: Frodo south from Hobbiton to the road (tile 62), Éomer's class-4 cavalry west across the plain of Rohan (tile 33), and Faramir's opening leg through the woods of North Ithilien (tiles 229 and 247, about 0.85 per axis on a diagonal) all show it. Which tiles count as road or track, and whether other terrain has a rate of its own, has not been recovered; paces here are sampled to about ±0.15 pixels a tick.
