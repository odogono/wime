## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-23

### Added

- **Click figures and the eye on the Animation Level.**
  Clicking a figure shows its status and selects it for the sack and the
  eye. The eye, or S, shows the selected Character's status: its health
  and what it carries, or a Unit's morale (#161).
- **Objects on the ground are drawn, and taking and putting down are
  animated.**
  On the Animation Level, objects lying where the watched Character stands
  appear in the scene. Take walks the Character to the object and back,
  and Put Down makes it stoop and leave the object at its feet (#162).
- **Clicking an object on the ground shows its name.**
  On the Animation Level, clicking an object's picture shows the object's
  name in a box at the top left, unless an object list is open. A figure
  in front of the picture still takes the click (#223).
- **The game can play itself as a Demo.** Choose Demo on the title
  menu, or leave the title menu alone for a minute, and one of three
  recorded campaigns plays from a New Game to its ending, with its fights,
  meetings and the Council at their usual pace. A Demo started from the
  menu lets you change the speed, move between Levels and look around
  with the magnifying glass, and you leave it through the Escape menu;
  one that started by itself goes back to the title at any key or click.
  A DEMO label shows throughout, and a Demo never touches your saves
  (#244).
- **Debug views.** In a dev build, or with `?debug` in the URL, Settings
  offers overlays on the Campaign Level: terrain costs, where Forces are
  heading, Encounter areas, Force labels and what is under the pointer.
- **Double-tap a map to go down a Level.** Double-clicking or double-tapping
  the Full Map Level opens the Campaign Level centred on that spot; doing
  the same on the Campaign Level opens the Animation Level, just as the
  magnifying glass does.
- **A Glass shows Evil setting lets the magnifying glass show Evil Forces.**
  The original's glass shows an empty spot when no Good Leader is in
  reach. With the setting on, it shows the first Evil Force's figures
  there instead, with time still standing.
- **A Settings menu.** The in-game menu's Settings switches sound off or on
  and shrinks the game to 75% or 50% of the window; both are remembered.

### Changed

- **A loaded game returns to the Level it was saved on, and the in-game
  menu stops every timer.**
  Loading opens the Animation Level on the Character it was watching, or
  the Campaign Level, instead of always the Ring-bearer. Messages and
  Gandalf's letter no longer run down while the in-game menu is open
  (#165).
- **Answer a chosen Demo's meeting and fight boxes.** "Go there" shows the
  place: a fight plays out in full, and a meeting goes down to the
  Animation Level. "Ignore it" and "Continue on" close the box. The Demo
  still plays on as it was recorded.
- **Giving an Order is quicker and confirmed.**
  Pressing the arrow with a Force's window open orders that Force
  straight away, and an Order once given shows where the Force is going.

### Fixed

- **The daily War Event check no longer runs on the Animation Level.**
  As in the original, an 8 a.m. that passes while the Animation Level is
  showing loses that day's check, so the dated events of days 149 and 169
  can be missed (#159).
- **The Council of Elrond moves followers to their destinations too.**
  As in the original, every entry with a destination is put at it, not
  only the Leaders (#163).
- **Game Speed looks and runs as in the original.**
  The hourglass opens the original's "Rate of Time Passage" window, with
  the current speed's button in red, and time holds while it is open.
  Very Hasty now advances a quarter-hour every 4 frames, the original's
  pace on an A500, rather than every frame (#164).
- **Each Good Leader in a fight now gets a row of its own.**
  As in the original, only followers and Evil Leaders merge into a row of
  the same name, so the infantry defending Minas Tirith, or the elves of
  Thranduil's Palace, fight as separate rows (#166).
- **Advisor talks, pass-bys and fights on the Animation Level now move as
  the original's figure scripts move them.**
  An advisor walks up to the party, speaks two boxes paced by its own
  script, and walks off; tips repeat once all twenty have been given.
  Figures passed by walk past the party while the clock stands. In a
  fight, each side walks to its line at its own pace, swings in place,
  recoils and lunges at a wound, and falls as the original's frames
  show, with rounds paced only by their messages (#169).
- **Clicking a button or an icon rings the bell, as in the original.**
  ReverbBells now rings as a click lands on any button or icon, and when
  the magnifying glass finds Forces near its spot (#170).
- **Galadriel's Mirror lists Forces as the original does.**
  Its Evil column now holds 20 rows, followers of followers are listed, a
  follower merges only with its own Leader's row, and a crowded Good column
  drops lone follower Characters before it is cut (#171).
- **Time on the Animation Level now follows the watched Character's walk.**
  As in the original, the world waits while the watched Character's
  figures cross the scene; each time they walk off it takes a step on
  the map and a new scene is built, until it arrives. With nobody watched
  time stops (#175).
- **The magnifying glass picks whom to watch as the original does.**
  It goes down on the lowest-numbered Good Leader near the spot, not the
  Force under the pointer, and over an Evil Force or bare ground it shows
  the spot with nobody watched (#176).
- **Message boxes open one at a time, in the order they are raised, and stay as long as the original's.** A box raised while another has just opened waits for it, the game standing still, instead of fights and meetings jumping the queue. Boxes without buttons stay up to about 24 seconds on the Campaign Level and 30 on the Animation Level unless a later one replaces them, leaving a Level closes its box, and the Full Map can't be opened under a box with buttons. A press off a box's buttons no longer closes it; a click on a Force or a Place replaces it, and N and Y answer it (#184).
- **Fights gather their rows as the original does.**
  A fight played on the Animation Level lists its rows again around the
  watched Character, with at most 9 a side. Fights against a balrog hold
  7 rows a side, the Evil side can hold one row more than its cap, an
  Encounter or Shelob fights alone, and Elrond keeps a fight on the
  Campaign Level (#187).
- **The combat window closes its lines up as the original does.**
  A fight played on the Animation Level or against a balrog uses 8-pixel
  lines once a side has more than 6 rows, not only above 16 (#187).
- **Pressed icons and buttons flash before they act, as in the original.**
  A click on an icon or a button now flashes it three times over half a
  second, and the magnifying glass flashes the Force it found (#196).
- **A Force takes an Order only on the original's roll.**
  Picking a Force to order now rolls against its allegiance, so Boromir or
  Gollum sometimes refuse, and an unmobilised Force always does, with "The
  force will not accept your orders." (#199).
- **The title screen's logo no longer shows black specks in browsers that
  guard against fingerprinting, such as Helium (#214).**
- **A damaged save is refused instead of loading as a broken game.**
  A save whose game state is missing or malformed now gets the "could not
  be loaded" message (#221).
- **Nazgûl reaching the Ford of Bruinen hide again.**
  As in the original, a Nazgûl whose orders end at the Ford vanishes from
  both maps and takes no part in fights until the Ring-bearer comes near (#228).
- **A Force parked on a Stronghold can be picked for an Order again.**
  Where Forces overlap, the Order arrow now picks the lowest-numbered Good
  Force under the click, as the original does, instead of the one drawn on
  top. Éomer at the Hornburg is picked over its Garrison, which never
  obeys (#230).
- **After the ending's last page, any key or click opens a menu to return to the title or load a save.**
  The original quits at this point. The port used to leave no way out
  except a browser reload. An ended game can no longer be resumed: its
  autosave is deleted when the ending starts (#231).
- **Hovering while paused no longer draws over the paused box.**
  On the Campaign Level, the name of the Force or Place under the pointer
  takes the place of "The Game is Paused." box instead of mixing with it,
  and the box returns when the pointer moves away (#232).
- **Q switches sound off and on on every Level.** It now works on the
  Animation and Full Map Levels too, not only on the Campaign Level (#236).
- **An Encounter that wins the Ring sends the 3rd Nazgûl to collect it.**
  He is revived even if asleep or dead, restored to full health and moved
  to the fight before carrying the Ring to Barad-dûr, so the Ring no
  longer disappears when an Encounter kills its bearer (#250).
- **The Campaign Level's right-side icons have transparent backgrounds.**
  The map shows through around their artwork instead of black rectangles.
- Draw Debug views beneath message windows so their text stays readable.
- **Settings in a Demo's menu.** The menu Escape opens in a chosen Demo now
  offers Settings between Resume and Return to title.
- **The paused box no longer shows behind other windows.**
  While the game is paused, a clicked Force's window, a notice or a hover
  name takes the paused box's place instead of drawing over it.
- **A key or click now skips every screen of the intro.** Clicks act as
  keys do, and the Ring verse no longer holds the screen until Title
  ends: a key or click on it opens the title menu.
