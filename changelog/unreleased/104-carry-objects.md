**Characters can put down and take objects, and the dead leave what they carried.** Objects lying on the map now live in the simulation's 30 placement slots from the start of the game: putting one down fills the first free slot, taking one frees it, taking the Ring makes the taker the Ring-bearer, and a Character killed in a fight drops everything it carried where it fell. Saved games keep objects carried and placed (#104).
