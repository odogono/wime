**Forces can head for a ford or bridge instead of wading a river.** The
simulation now has a Movement rule: under Crossing movement, a Force
whose way meets a river makes first for a Crossing within reach and
crosses there. Classic movement, the original's, is still the default
and is kept exactly; saves from before load as Classic movement. Choosing
the rule on the New Game screen follows (#332).
