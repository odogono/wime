**Mordor is defended by the Morannon (the Black Gate), with Carach Angren as a second line behind it, while Cirith Ungol is "not well guarded".**
Cirith Ungol is the pass through the Mountains of Shadow that Gollum used to leave Mordor. The manual's glossary places Carach Angren in the north-west corner of Mordor, but its entry for the Morannon says north-east; the north-west is the usual reading.
