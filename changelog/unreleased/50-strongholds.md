**The war can now be lost, and won, on the battlefield.** If the Garrisons of three of the five Strongholds (Minas Tirith, Lórien, Thranduil's Palace, Edoras and the Hornburg) are destroyed, you lose the war. If the great Orc Garrison of Barad-dûr is destroyed, you win. Games saved before this release can't be restored (#50).
