**The Full Map Level shows the whole playing area, with every Force drawn as a coloured dot: blinking blue for the player's, red for Evil and green for Neutral.**
The manual lists four icons on this Level: the eye (status), the scroll (archive, for saving and restoring), the magnifying glass (down to the Campaign Level) and the hourglass (Game Speed). The glossary definition mentions only blue and red; the manual adds green for Neutral Forces and says the player's dots blink.
