**Fights gather their rows as the original does.**
A fight played on the Animation Level lists its rows again around the
watched Character, with at most 9 a side. Fights against a balrog hold
7 rows a side, the Evil side can hold one row more than its cap, an
Encounter or Shelob fights alone, and Elrond keeps a fight on the
Campaign Level (#187).
