**The Campaign Level shows an area of about 250 by 150 miles, scrolls when the pointer touches an edge, and draws each Force as the figure of its Leader.**
All terrain is shown: woods, hills, rivers and settlements. Scrolling continues while the pointer stays at the edge and stops when it moves away. When Characters follow a Leader, only the Leader's figure appears. Forces the player can control flash. Clicking a Character or Place opens a window at the top left naming it. The icons here are the eye (status), the map (up a Level), the magnifying glass (down a Level), the hourglass (Game Speed) and the arrow (Orders).
