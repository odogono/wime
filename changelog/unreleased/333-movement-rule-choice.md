**Forces now cross rivers at fords and bridges, and the Atlas map is the
default.** New games are played under Crossing movement: a Force whose
way meets a river heads first for a nearby ford or bridge instead of
stalling on the bank. The New Game screen has a Movement choice beside
the Setup, so Classic movement, the original's, is one choice away, and
the Load lists name a save played under Crossing movement. Saves from
before load as Classic movement, and the Demo still plays the original's
way. The map now starts as the Atlas map for players who have not
chosen one; Map: Classic in Settings keeps the original's look (#324).
