**The game starts with only Frodo, Sam and Pippin under the player's control, carrying the Ring, while the Nazgûl head for the Shire.**
Their first goal is Rivendell, along the Great East Road past Bree. Other Characters, such as Merry and Aragorn, join later as they are met.
