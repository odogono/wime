**The music appears to be IFF SMUS scores with separate instrument files, spread over both disks.**
Both disk images hold files ending `.smus` (scores), `.instr` (instruments) and `.ss` (sampled sounds). Disk 1 has title, victory and defeat pieces such as `title.smus`, `ultvict.smus` and `shortdefeat.smus`. Disk 2 has themes named for Characters and Places, such as `frodo.smus`, `gandalf.smus`, `aragorn.smus`, `rohanians.smus`, `minastirith.b.smus` and `hob.riven.smus`. The format is judged from the file names only; none of these files has been decoded.
