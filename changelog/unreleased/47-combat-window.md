**Choose how to fight.** When a fight starts you are told who is attacked and can "Go there" or "Ignore it". Going there opens the combat window over the map, where you set Charge, Engage, Withdraw or Retreat for each of your Characters and Units as the losses are reported round by round. Ignoring it, or not answering within about 14 seconds, settles the fight at once and tells you the result and who died (#47).
